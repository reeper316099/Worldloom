## Explanation

Adepts strengthen the [[Cores|core]] in their chest through seven realms: [[Spark]], [[Ember]], [[Cinder]], Flame, Blaze, Pyre and Zenith.

## Rules

1. Each realm needs a fresh trial.
2. The [[Ember Ledger]] records the result.
3. Skipping a realm is possible but dangerous.

## Loopholes

{{claim: theory}}The Pyre Oath may bypass the third rule.{{/claim}}
